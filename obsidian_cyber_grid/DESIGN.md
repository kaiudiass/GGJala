## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-tier, authoritative, and frictionless marketplace identity engineered specifically for high-stakes digital assets: competitive gaming profiles, rare digital collectibles, in-game liquidity, and premium AI compute seats.

### Target Audience & Emotional Arc
The user base encompasses discerning digital native power-users, seasoned esports competitors, indie creators, and AI engineers. The platform shifts the perception of secondary digital commerce from shadowy, unreliable forums to an institutional-grade, secure, and impeccably swift exchange. The interface inspires confidence, precision, velocity, and uncompromised escrow trust through pristine technical clarity.

### Design Movement: Hyper-Dark Precision Glass
Departing from cluttered, neon-soaked gaming tropes, this design system synthesizes deep obsidian minimalism with ultra-fine glassmorphism and surgical emerald luminescence:
- **Void Obsidian Bases:** Multi-tier deep carbon backdrops (`#0a0c10`, `#11141a`, `#161b22`) create infinite visual depth, eliminating visual noise and drawing full focus to product media.
- **Electric Emerald Precision:** Sparing, high-frequency kinetic green highlights (`#00e676`, `#10b981`) serve exclusively as functional anchors—signaling verified reputations, active inventory status, secure transactions, and instant conversions.
- **Micro-Bevel Glass Surfaces:** Semi-translucent panels bordered by sub-pixel hairline edges (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`) provide structured tactile separation without visual weight.

## Colors

The palette operates on a strict functional hierarchy built on low-luminance substrates and surgical accent spikes.

### Base & Background Layers
- **Canvas Base (`#0a0c10`):** The absolute foundational background, absorbing ambient light and framing content.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#11141a`):** Primary card bodies, navigation bars, and grouped containers.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#161b22`):** Interactive container states, nested table headers, and modal envelopes.
- **Surface Elevation 3 (`#1f242d`):** Elevated popovers, flyout menus, and active input cavities.

### Accent & Status Matrix
- **Primary / Cyber Emerald (`#00e676`):** Reserved for core transactional affordances, active state dots, positive balance indicators, and highest-priority CTA buttons.
- **Secondary / Deep Emerald (`#10b981`):** Subdued success indicators, trust tags, verified seller badges, and secondary affirmative chips.
- **Tertiary / Glacial Cyan (`#38bdf8`):** AI tier identifications, subscription status, and automated instant-delivery indicators.
- **Warning / Amber Flare (`#f59e0b`):** Pending approvals, escrow hold periods, and limited-supply alerts.
- **Destructive / Neon Crimson (`#ef4444`):** Disputed trades, security warnings, and negative feedback marks.

### Monochrome & Text Tiers
- **Text High-Emphasis (`#f8fafc`):** Listing titles, numerical asset balances, and pricing values.
- **Text Medium-Emphasis (`#94a3b8`):** Metadata, descriptive specs, inactive navigation items, and secondary labels.
- **Text Low-Emphasis (`#475569`):** Watermarks, disabled indicators, and structural timestamp entries.
- **Borders & Seams (`#22272e` / `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`): 1px structural partitions providing separation across overlapping elements.

## Typography

The typographic strategy pairs mechanical precision with uncompromised human readability.

### Structural Font Alignment
- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Delivers a technical, cutting-edge edge suited for gaming titles, high-tier categories, and financial pricing metrics. Characterized by sharp apertures and tight tracking.
- **Body & Continuous Copy (`Inter`):** Handles long-form listing explanations, terms of transfer, seller profiles, and support dialogues with zero geometric distraction.
- **Data & Verification Badges (`JetBrains Mono`):** Applied to monetary sums, transaction IDs, security hashes, latency markers, and verified trust tags. Gives transactional data an authentic, terminal-grade assurance.

### Style Directives
- Numeric pricing strings must always be styled with tabular figures (`tnum`) in `Space Grotesk` or `JetBrains Mono` to prevent layout shift during price flickers or countdown refreshes.
- Section tags and verification metadata render in uppercase using `label-sm` or `label-md` with tracking between `+0.04em` and `+0.06em`.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on an intentional 8pt spatial grid designed for modular density, ensuring high information bandwidth without visual clutter.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (`>1024px`) with a maximum container bounding width of `1380px`. 8-column layout on tablet (`768px - 1023px`), and 4-column stack on mobile (`<767px`).
- **Outer Bounds:** `margin` defines edge-padding for desktop viewports (`2rem`), reducing smoothly to `1rem` on narrow mobile clients.
- **Content Channels:** Product grids adopt uniform `gutter` rules (`1.25rem` on desktop, scaling to `0.75rem` on mobile) to maintain dense, scannable catalog walls.

### Responsive Behaviors
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Listings display in 4-to-5 column responsive card grids. Split screens for listing detail pages maintain a 60/40 ratio between visual showcase/specs and fixed escrow checkout sidebars.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Sidebars convert into horizontal collapsible accordions. Item grids adapt to 3 columns.
- **Mobile (<767px):** Full-bleed responsive cards with horizontal overflow snap-carousels for trending categories and featured drops. Checkout blocks dock to a persistent bottom drawer.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on light-emitting edges and dark-field surfaces rather than high-offset shadows, which would muddy deep dark interfaces.

### Surface Stratification
- **Ground Zero (Background):** Pure matte `#0a0c10`, non-reflective, flat.
- **Level 1 (Card Matrix):** Semi-transparent `#11141a` backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Outlined by a crisp `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`. Hover states raise outline luminosity to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` alongside a subtle emerald reflection (`0 0 20px rgba(0, 230, 118, 0.08)`).
- **Level 2 (Floating Escrow / Modals):** Background elevated to `#161b22`, reinforced with an ultra-soft dual shadow:
  - Ambient base: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`
  - Keylight perimeter: `0 0 0 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
- **Level 3 (Tooltips & Verified Popovers):** Solid `#1f242d` with a sharp `0 8px 16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)` drop and a slight top rim-light `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`.

### Glass & Glow Effects
- **Emerald Glow Tracing:** Active badges, buy buttons, and online status pings project a focused ambient bloom: `box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(0, 230, 118, 0.35)`.
- **Frosting:** Use `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` on the sticky top navigation header, paired with `background: rgba(10, 12, 16, 0.8)`.

## Shapes

The geometric identity balances industrial precision with modern ergonomic comfort. 

### Corner Radii Hierarchy
- **Standard Controls & Chips (0.5rem / 8px):** Buttons, search fields, category tabs, and status pills. Delivers clean, purposeful touch targets that avoid both retro-brutalist harshness and overly playful bubble curves.
- **Cards & Enclosures (1rem / 16px):** Outer listing cards, seller identity panels, spec grids, and modal frames.
- **Pill Tags (Full / 9999px):** Dedicated exclusively to compact status indicators, including `VERIFIED SELLER`, `INSTANT DELIVERY`, and online/offline presence dots.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Transactional:** High-luminance emerald fill (`#00e676`), pure black text (`#05150c`), `Space Grotesk` weight 600, corner radius `0.5rem`. Hover triggers a brightness bump (`#1bf789`) and an electric halo (`box-shadow: 0 0 18px rgba(0, 230, 118, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary Glass:** Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, text `#f8fafc`. Hover transition shifts fill to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` and border to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)`.
- **Ghost Utility:** Borderless, text `#94a3b8`, used for secondary icons (cart, favorite, share). Hover renders text in `#f8fafc` over a faint `#161b22` wash.

### Trust & Identity Badges
- **Verified Seller Shield:** Compact pill containing a miniature check icon alongside monospace label (`JetBrains Mono`, 11px). Filled with `rgba(0, 230, 118, 0.12)`, text `#00e676`, bordered by `1px solid rgba(0, 230, 118, 0.25)`.
- **Escrow Guarantee Seal:** Dark panel badge with a hairline green gradient edge, housing a secure lock icon and the text "Proteção ggJala".
- **Rank/Tier Insignia:** Diamond and tier indicators (e.g., Diamante, Platina) featuring tinted neon fills (glacier blue for AI, radiant purple for rare game accounts) with matching edge illumination.

### Cards & Grid Containers
- **Product Listing Card:** Outer wrapper uses `#11141a` backed by glass blur, 1px `#22272e` border, and `1rem` corner rounding.
  - **Media Area:** 16:9 banner container with an inner inset border to frame screenshots. Hover triggers a subtle image scale (`scale(1.03)`).
  - **Meta Rows:** High-contrast price (`Space Grotesk`, bold, `#f8fafc`), monospace stock count (`#94a3b8`), seller reputation rating with a star or level icon, and a rapid-delivery tag.
  - **Interactive State:** Hover lifts the card by 2px with an edge shift toward primary emerald tint (`rgba(0, 230, 118, 0.2)`).

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Market Search Engine:** Deep obsidian input trough (`#0d1015`) inset with `box-shadow: inset 0 2px 4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`, enclosed by a subtle 1px border (`#22272e`). Integrated monospace keyboard shortcut tag (`Press / to search`). Active focus applies a crisp, focused green border (`#00e676`) without offset rings.
- **Form Controls:** Text inputs feature inset labels that collapse gracefully into small uppercase `label-sm` positions on focus.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes, Toggles & Radios)
- **Checkboxes:** 18x18px rounded square (`4px` radius) with a dark chassis (`#161b22`) and a fine perimeter seam (`#2d333b`). Selected state fills with `#00e676` and an obsidian check glyph.
- **Toggles:** Pill-shaped slider with a muted `#161b22` track and a `#475569` thumb. Active state ignites the track into `#10b981` with a luminous `#f8fafc` thumb.
